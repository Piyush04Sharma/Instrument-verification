How verification works

  1. Register the instrument. Shopkeepers add the make, serial number, capacity and accuracy class.
  2. Apply and pay. Upload a licence or ID proof and pay the fee for the instrument's category.
  3. Inspection. An officer reviews the documents, schedules a visit and records the readings.
  4. Certificate. If the readings are within the permissible error, a certificate with a QR code is issued.

Who is it for

Shopkeepers and business owners register instruments, track applications and download certificates.

Inspection officers review applications, schedule inspections and issue certificates.

Administrators manage users, categories and reports.
